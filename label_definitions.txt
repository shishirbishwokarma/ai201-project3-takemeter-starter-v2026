evidence: the post backs its point with a specific fact a reader could check, such as a study, a number, a date, a named rule or a named source (a checkable fact counts even with no source named; a prescription like "rest 2-3 minutes" or numbers about the poster's own training do not)
claim: the post gives advice or an opinion with confidence but offers nothing checkable and no personal experience behind it, only the poster's say-so
personal: the post's support, or the whole post, is the poster's own training, body or experience, "I did X and Y happened"
